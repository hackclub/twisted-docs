# What is Twisted?
Twisted is a unique YSWS where you make cool projects and complete a twisted roadmap full of challenges to get PC!

# Who can participate?
Twisted and all other [Hack Club](https://hackclub.com/) YSWSes are open to anyone aged 13-18! All the teenagers, anywhere in the world can participate!
