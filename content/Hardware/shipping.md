# Heres more about shipping hardware projects to Twisted:
## 1. Document what your project actually is
Add a bit about what your project actually is to your repo. Stuff like these matter more than you think:

- Why did you build it? Your motivation and story.
- A short description of what your project actually does
- A brief of how it all fits together. Stuff like photos, wiring diagrams, PCBs etc. of your design.

## 2. Understandable and organised.
Make all files and resources easily accessible and organised. It should be customizable. (Include all source files & step files!)

When your project is nothing but a dump of files and two sentences for a README, its **hard for others to recognize your work, to learn from it**. _It’s not real_. It only lives on in your tiny corner of this earth.

## 3. Public, and online.
Put it on a public repository on any git hosting service!

You can use [GitHub](https://github.com), [GitLab](https://gitlab.com), [CodeBerg](https://codeberg.org/), [Nest Git](https://git.hackclub.app), or even your own instance!
